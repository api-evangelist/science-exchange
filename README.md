Science Exchange is an R&D supplier orchestration and procurement platform for life-sciences organizations. It runs a marketplace of 3,800+ pre-qualified scientific service providers under a single master service agreement, and streamlines supplier sourcing, onboarding, contracting, compliance, and payment. The platform integrates with ERP (SAP, Oracle, Workday), P2P (Ariba, Coupa), and SSO/SAML identity systems, and exposes a read-only Providers REST API (v1) that lets scientific-service suppliers pull their RFQs, quotes, services, facilities, line items, ratings, tags, and users. Founded in 2011 and headquartered in Palo Alto, California; backed by a16z, Index Ventures, and Union Square Ventures.

**Areas:** Company · Life Sciences · Research and Development · Scientific Services · Procurement · Marketplace · Supplier Management · Biotechnology · Pharmaceuticals

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Science Exchange: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.

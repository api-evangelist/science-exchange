Most life sciences teams outsource health economics and outcomes research the same way every cycle: a scientist or market access lead finds a vendor, procurement scrambles to contract, and quality chases qualification paperwork after the work is already underway. The science gets done. The process around it leaks time and money that no one fully measures.

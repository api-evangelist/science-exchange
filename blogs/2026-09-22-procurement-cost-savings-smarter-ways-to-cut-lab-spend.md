Bundling purchases is one of the simplest ways to stretch a budget further. Multiple single-item orders rack up shipping and handling fees that add up fast if you're not paying attention, so it's worth evaluating your purchasing patterns and consolidating where you can. Many vendors also offer free shipping minimums or promos worth watching for.
